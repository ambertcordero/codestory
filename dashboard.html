Processing the Order

From cart to confirmation — how the system validates the order, updates the inventory, and prepares the receipt.

System Story 5 min read Apr 24, 2025 Payment Module

The Story Behind the Transaction

When a customer confirms the order, the system checks whether the payment is sufficient. Once validated, the transaction proceeds to update the inventory and generate a receipt.

This process ensures that every sale is recorded accurately, stock levels are updated in real-time, and the customer receives a proper receipt for their purchase.
